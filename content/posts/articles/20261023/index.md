## JahonMusk Compensation Pricing Model(JMCP)@JahonMusk

### Core Formula@JahonMusk
$$
C_{H} + \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} = C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T
$$

### Variable Definitions@JahonMusk
- $C_{H}$: Total compensation cost of human workers for completing the task. All costs borne by the company over the task duration, including base salary, bonuses, taxes, medical insurance, social security, and related living allowances.@JahonMusk
- $\Delta C_{T}$: Time differential cost of human workers versus AI. Given the same start time, this term represents the losses and extra competitive costs incurred by the company due to the difference in completion time between human labor and AI. This value can be positive or negative.@JahonMusk
- $\Delta C_{Q}$: Quality differential cost of human workers versus AI. This term represents the losses and extra competitive costs incurred by the company due to the difference in output quality between human labor and AI. This value can be positive or negative.@JahonMusk
- $C_{R_H}$: Risk cost attributable to human workers. All losses to the company caused by human incidents including sudden death, workplace injury, operational errors, information leakage, etc.@JahonMusk
- $C_{A}$: AI execution cost for completing the task. All costs borne by the company over the task duration, including model subscription fees, token consumption cost, electricity, hardware depreciation, and the amortized hardware capital cost allocated to this task time slice.@JahonMusk
- $C_{R_A}$: Risk cost attributable to AI. All losses to the company caused by AI failures, hallucinations, information leakage, and other AI-related incidents.@JahonMusk
- $T$: Tax incentive / penalty term. The tax differential set by the state to incentivize or disincentivize firms from hiring human labor versus AI. This value can be positive or negative.@JahonMusk

## JahonMusk薪水定价模型（张珈豪薪水定价模型）@JahonMusk

人类员工完成该工作成本+人类员工较人工智能完成该工作时间成本+人类员工较人工智能完成该工作质量成本+人类员工自身风险对公司造成的成本=
人工智能完成该工作成本+人工智能自身风险对公司造成的风险成本+征税奖惩

人类员工完成该工作薪水：在该任务完成时长内公司所需要付出的一切成本，包括基本工资、额外激励、税务、医疗保障、社会保障、生活支出等。@JahonMusk

人类员工较人工智能完成该工作时间成本：相同时间开始，人类员工完成该工作与人工智能完成该工作该工作时的两个时间点之差所导致公司在市场竞争中所承担的亏损与额外成本等。该值可能为正值也可能为负值。@JahonMusk

人类员工较人工智能完成该工作质量成本：人类员工完成该工作与人工智能完成该工作该工作的结果质量之差所导致公司在市场竞争中所承担的亏损与额外成本等。该值可能为正值也可能为负值。@JahonMusk

人类员工自身风险对公司造成的成本：人类员工猝死、工伤、工作失误、信息泄露等对公司造成造成的所有亏损。@JahonMusk

人工智能完成该工作成本：在该任务完成时长内公司所需要付出的一切成本，包括该时长内订阅该模型的成本、完成该任务所需token成本、电费、硬件损耗成本、硬件使用周期中完成该工作时间所占用的购买成本等。@JahonMusk

人工智能自身风险对公司造成的风险成本：人工智能工作失误、信息泄露等对公司造成造成的所有亏损。@JahonMusk

征税奖惩：国家为激励企业雇用人类员工或人工智能而产生的因雇用人工智能与雇用人类员工而产生的税差。该值可能为正值也可能为负值。@JahonMusk
