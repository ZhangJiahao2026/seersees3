# 我（张珈豪）的理想
# My(ZhangJiahao's) Ideal

我的理想是以各种形式推动人类各方面发展，为人类文明长久延续做出贡献。

My ideal is to promote human development in all aspects in various forms and contribute to the long-term continuation of human civilization.

## 我（张珈豪）认为当前我的潜在实现人生价值方式或方向
## My(ZhangJiahao's) Potential Paths and Directions for Realizing Personal Value

1. 
我（张珈豪）认为当前人类在医学、生理学和生物学等一切与生物有关的研究仍停留在初级阶段，根本原因在于人类在对生物体内微观世界的研究条件、研究方法与研究工具方面仍然落后。

I(ZhangJiahao) believe that current human research in medicine, physiology, biology and all other biology-related fields is still in its early stages. The fundamental reason is that humans still lag behind in the research conditions, methodologies and tools for exploring the microscopic world inside living organisms.

我（张珈豪）希望能够在人类在对生物体内微观世界的研究条件、研究方法与研究工具方面做出贡献，以此减少病痛对人类的折磨，提升人类平均寿命，推动脑科学的研究。

I(ZhangJiahao) hope to contribute to the research conditions, methodologies and tools for exploring the microscopic world inside living organisms. By doing so, I aim to reduce human suffering caused by illnesses, raise the average human lifespan, and advance brain science research.

2. 
我（张珈豪）认为由于人工智能与机器人的发展，人们会有更多时间参与到各领域的创作与创造，从中思考、寻找、追求自己人生的意义，或从中获取快乐；同时人们也会有更多时间通过别人的创作与创造，思考、寻找、找到自己人生的意义，或从中获取快乐。

I(ZhangJiahao) believe that with the development of artificial intelligence and robotics, people will have more time to engage in creation and innovation across various fields. They can reflect on, seek after, and pursue the meaning of their lives, or find joy in the process. Meanwhile, people will also have more time to reflect on, seek and discover the meaning of their lives, or gain happiness through the creations of others.

我（张珈豪）希望建立一个能够让更多人找到、实现自己人生的意义或获取无害的快乐的环境或平台。

I(ZhangJiahao) hope to build an environment or platform where more people can discover and fulfill the meaning of their lives or attain harmless happiness.

3. 
我（张珈豪）认为当前大众在信息的传递与理解方法上存在不足，信息在传递时不可避免地出现摩擦（多人在使用非标准化语言（暂称）时传递信息时，演讲者表达的含义与聆听者理解的含义的偏差），这种情况在传递关键信息时是非常不希望被看到的。

I(ZhangJiahao) believe the general public currently has deficiencies in the ways information is transmitted and understood. Friction inevitably occurs during information transfer — when multiple people communicate using what I tentatively call non-standardized language, there is a discrepancy between what the speaker intends to express and what the listener perceives. This outcome is highly undesirable when conveying critical information.

我（张珈豪）希望创造一种具有可验证正误、可量化、标准化特性的新语言，帮助人们在有需要时，尽可能消除信息传递时的摩擦。当然这不会影响人类社会原有的文化多样性，我希望这门语言主要作为工具存在。

I(ZhangJiahao) hope to create a new language with verifiable correctness, quantifiability and standardization. It will help people eliminate information friction as much as possible when needed. Of course, this will not undermine the existing cultural diversity of human society. I intend for this language to function primarily as a tool.
