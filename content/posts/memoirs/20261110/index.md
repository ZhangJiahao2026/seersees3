现在，出轨、监管、法案，跳楼的跳楼，卧轨的卧轨，看起来很悲观了。就连在抖音上讲在美国生活的宝妈都开始抱怨黄仁勋这一档子事影响到她自己持有的英伟达了，这让我不得不开始关注起来。不过，我认为还不够悲观。

现在的价格对我的诱惑还是不够大，没有那种“入场即收尸”的感觉，换句话说就是：“死的人还不够多！”

原因主要还是这一次上涨主要是靠存储撑起来的，而我一直在思考存储撑起的上涨算不算是“最后一牛”？是不是炒别的已经没用了，只能靠这个了？

看一下K线图可知，靠存储撑起来的上涨是在2026年4月起，在此之前SOXL就已经来到了历史高点，存储不过是让它来到了前所未有的高度。而其实在此之前从2025年4月到2026年3月的上涨属于是自然上涨。

不过谈论这些都没有用。紧盯情绪，紧盯点位。我现在就期待两件事，一件是SOXL来到7块（5个月前301.802块，此时此刻51.730块），一件是投资者鬼哭狼嚎、哭天喊地，不过这还不够，最好是喝药、上吊、遇刺、跳楼、卧轨、暗杀，全市场情绪崩溃、所有人都说“我操他妈的AI彻底完了芯片彻底完了人类彻底完了世界他妈的要毁灭了！”。这种时候才算得上收尸，才算得上是抄底，才算得上是真正的长期主义、耐心资本。

每个人一生都会遇到为数不多的几次属于自己的巨浪，“三年不开张，开张吃三辈子”说的保守了，必须吃“三百辈子”。不过归根到底还是命，还是自己能不能把握住。

最后，也是最重要的，AI和芯片一定有价值，这是我最清醒的时候说的，是我用来提醒我自己的。如果有一天市场情绪悲观到了极点，什么“AI完了，芯片完了，人类要灭绝了，地球要毁灭了”这样的话都出来了，我也糊涂地说“哎呀，这个AI和芯片可能真不行，真没什么价值”，那我应该再翻出来这句话，反复阅读，不需要什么思考，只需要做出违背人性的决定：坚定地买入半导体并坚定持有，直到所有人都说：“人类能永生，人类是神！”。



Right now — derailments, regulatory crackdowns, legislation; some people jumping off buildings, some lying down on train tracks — it all looks pretty pessimistic. Even the stay-at-home mom on Douyin who talks about life in the US has started complaining that this whole Jensen Huang business is affecting her NVIDIA holdings. That forced me to start paying attention. But I still think it’s not pessimistic enough.

The current price still isn’t tempting enough for me — it doesn’t have that “enter and immediately start collecting corpses” feeling. In other words: “Not enough people have died yet!”

The main reason is that this rally has mainly been propped up by memory chips. And I keep thinking: does a rally propped up by memory count as the “last bull”? Is betting on anything else already useless — is this all we’ve got left?

Looking at the candlestick chart, the memory-driven rally began in April 2026. Before that, SOXL had already reached an all-time high; memory just took it to an unprecedented level. And actually, the rally from April 2025 to March 2026 was a natural rally.

But talking about all this is useless. Keep your eyes glued to sentiment; keep your eyes glued to price levels. Right now I’m hoping for two things: one, SOXL drops to 7 — five months ago it was 301.802, right now it’s 51.730; and two, investors wailing and crying, howling at the sky. But that’s still not enough — ideally, drinking poison, hanging themselves, getting assassinated, jumping off buildings, lying on train tracks, being assassinated — the entire market’s sentiment collapsing, everyone saying, “AI is totally fucked, chips are totally fucked, humanity is totally fucked, the fucking world is going to end!” Only at a time like that does it count as collecting corpses, count as bottom-fishing, count as true long-termism and patient capital.

Everyone in their life encounters a few huge waves that belong to them. “Three years without a sale, one sale feeds you for three lifetimes” is too conservative — it has to feed you for “three hundred lifetimes.” But in the end, it’s still fate — whether you can seize it or not.

Finally, and most importantly: AI and chips definitely have value. This is what I said when I was at my clearest — it’s what I use to remind myself. If one day market sentiment becomes extremely pessimistic, and things like “AI is finished, chips are finished, humanity is going extinct, the Earth is going to be destroyed” start coming out, and I, confused, also say, “Oh man, maybe AI and chips really are no good, really have no value,” then I should pull out this sentence again and read it over and over. No thinking needed — just make the decision that goes against human nature: firmly buy semiconductors and firmly hold, until everyone says, “Humans can live forever; humans are gods!”
