Jahon Musk

我认为今天是历史性的一刻——我为自己取了英文名：

Jahon Zhang Musk

I consider this a historic moment, for I have selected my English name:

Jahon Zhang Musk

First name选用Jahon来自我的中文名字“珈豪”的发音。

Middle name选用Zhang来自我的中文姓氏“张”的汉语拼音。

Last name选用Musk是向Elon Musk致敬。

My first name, Jahon, is derived from the pronunciation of my Chinese given name Jiahao(珈豪).

My middle name, Zhang, comes from the pinyin of my Chinese surname Zhang(张).

My last name, Musk, is a tribute to Elon Musk.
