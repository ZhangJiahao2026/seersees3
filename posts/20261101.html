Inferences Based on the JMCP Model: The Working Mode of Human Employees(基于张珈豪薪水定价模型的推论：人类员工的工作模式)

Learn about the JMCP Model

Core Formula of the JMCP Model

$$ C_{H} + \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} = C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T $$

Assumption 1: Every company must need human employees

Basis for the assumption (only one of the following needs to hold)

1. Humans can play an irreplaceable role in bearing fault liability.

When an error occurs, fault liability and various forms of adjudication must be traced to a natural person.

Conditions for holding: this is based on the need to assign liability when errors occur and the inability of AI to bear responsibility (e.g., laws or company requirements mandate that humans bear responsibility).

Scenario in which it fails: responsibility is borne by the company owning the AI, relevant insurance companies, or other non-human entities, and the law does not trace errors made by AI to individuals.

2. Policies and rules favor companies hiring human employees.

The prerequisites for company establishment and development (such as listing thresholds), policy requirements (such as human employees being one of the conditions for meeting a certain threshold), and policy incentives (such as tax reductions or exemptions) favor companies that hire more human employees.

Conditions for holding: the will of the state is that "the people should work to maintain individual stability (avoid social unrest)."

Scenario in which it fails: the state decides to use other means to maintain individual stability, such as entertainment.

Conditions for the failure scenario to hold: a more suitable means of stabilizing individuals that the state can control emerges.

3. There are jobs that can only be done by humans.

AI instruction issuer? Final confirmer of AI output results?

Conditions for holding: there are always jobs that AI and robots cannot perform, or whose cost is higher than hiring humans, and thus require humans to complete.

Scenario in which it fails: AI and robots can accurately complete all work, and the cost of employing AI and robots to complete the work is lower than the cost of hiring humans to complete it.

Conditions for the failure scenario to hold: AI and robots develop to the point where, when completing the same work, the electricity and other costs of using AI and robots are lower than the cost of food and water required to sustain human life.

Assumption 2: The number of humans willing to work is greater than the number of jobs

Basis for the assumption (all must hold simultaneously)

1. Basic living necessities still require payment.

Food, water, housing, and other basic living necessities, or the materials required for other forms of human life support developed by technology, still need to be exchanged for money.

Conditions for holding: the monetary system still exists and human life support still requires costs.

2. The structure of wealth distribution does not undergo sudden changes.

There will be no situation in which distribution methods other than distribution according to work become absolutely dominant, and labor in the job market is sufficient.

Deduction:

New variable:

Human life-sustaining cost $C_{L}$: the purchase cost of food, water, housing, and other basic living necessities, or the materials required for other forms of human life support developed by technology. This value is less than $C_{H}$ and greater than 0.

Relationship between human employee salaries and AI costs based on the JMCP Model:

$$ C_{H} = C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T - ( \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} ) $$

$$ C_{L} < C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T - ( \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} ) $$

Inference:

In my view:

Under the premise that $\Delta C_{T}$ and $\Delta C_{Q}$ are positive by default and will grow over time (with AI development), $C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T$ will inevitably grow.

$C_{A}$ reflects the will of AI companies, $C_{R_A}$ reflects the capabilities of AI models, and $T$ reflects the will of the state.

$C_{R_A}$ will fluctuate over time and is difficult to predict.

My core conclusion is:

Among the three — “$C_{A}$ increases,” “$T$ increases,” and “$C_{A}$ decreases” — at least one will occur in the future.

了解JMCP Model

JMCP Model核心公式

$$ C_{H} + \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} = C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T $$

假设1：任何公司一定需要人类员工

假设依据（其中一条成立即可）

1.人类能够起到不可替代的、承担过错责任的作用。

出现错误时，过错责任与各类审判必须追究至自然人。

成立前提：基于出现错误需要追责且人工智能无法承担责任（法律或公司要求必须人类承担责任等）。

失效情景：责任由人工智能所属公司或相关保险公司或其他非人单位承担，且法律不会将人工智能出现的错误追究到个人。

2.政策与规则倾向公司应当雇用人类员工。

企业成立、发展前提（如上市门槛等）或政策要求（如人类员工作为满足某个门槛的条件之一）、政策优惠（如减税免税等）倾向于帮助雇用更多人类员工的公司。

成立前提：国家意志是“百姓应当工作以保持个体稳定（避免社会动荡）”

失效情景：国家决定采用其他方式保持个体稳定，如娱乐。

失效情景成立前提：出现了更合适的、国家能够控制的、用来稳定个体的方式。

3.存在只能由人完成的工作。

人工智能指令发出员？人工智能输出结果最终确认员？

成立前提：总有工作存在人工智能与机器人不能胜任或成本高于雇佣人类的情况，需要人类完成。

失效情景：人工智能与机器人能精确完成一切工作且雇用人工智能与机器人完成该工作所需的费用低于雇佣人类完成该工作的费用。

失效情景成立前提：人工智能与机器人发展且完成相同工作时使用人工智能与机器人的电费等低于人类维持生命所需食物与水的费用。

假设2：愿意参与工作的人类数量大于工作岗位数量

假设依据（需要同时成立）

1.基本生活物资仍需付费

食物、水、住房等基本生活物资或科技发展出其他的人类生命维持形式的所需的物资仍需要钱兑换。

成立前提：货币体系仍然存在且人类生命维持仍需成本等。

2.财富分配方式结构不发生骤变

不会出现除非按劳分配外的各种分配方式占绝对主导的情况，就业市场中的劳动力充足。

推演：

新变量：

人类维持生命成本 $C_{L}$ ：食物、水、住房等基本生活物资或科技发展出其他的人类生命维持形式的所需的物资购买成本。此值小于 $C_{H}$ 且大于0。

基于JMCP Model的人类员工薪资与人工智能成本间的关系：

$$ C_{H} = C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T - ( \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} ) $$

$$ C_{L} < C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T - ( \Delta C_{T} + \Delta C_{Q} + C_{R_H} ) $$

推论：

我认为：

在 $\Delta C_{T}$ 与 $\Delta C_{Q}$ 默认是正值且会随时间推移而增长（人工智能发展）的前提下， $C_{A} + C_{R_A} + T$ 必然增长。

$C_{A}$ 是人工智能公司意志的体现，$C_{R_A}$ 是人工智能模型能力的体现， $T$ 是国家意志的体现。

$C_{R_A}$ 会随着时间推移而波动，且难以预测。

我得出的核心结论是：

“ $C_{A}$ 增长”、“ $T$ 增长”、“ $C_{A}$ 降低”这三项中，未来至少会发生一项。
